# Credits

Masterplan's build and discovery practices adapt ideas, rewritten in its own words, from two MIT-licensed skill collections:

- **Superpowers** by Jesse Vincent (https://github.com/obra/superpowers): task plans with interfaces, global constraints and a review focus; a fresh implementer per task with a separate task review and a capped fix loop; a ledger that survives context loss; rulings instead of stalls; test-first discipline; root-cause debugging; evidence before completion claims; the builder check.
- **Matt Pocock's skills** (https://github.com/mattpocock/skills): question rounds over a decision tree with a recommended answer for each; facts found by the agent rather than asked; the three-part test for when a decision deserves an ADR; glossaries with terms to avoid; deep modules and seams; tracer-bullet slices with blocking edges and expand–contract for wide changes; two-axis code review; the debugging feedback loop; guided scripts for steps only a person can do.

Both collections are released under the MIT License (Copyright (c) Jesse Vincent; Copyright (c) Matt Pocock). Masterplan borrows their ideas and none of their text or code, so their license files aren't bundled here. The credit is because the ideas are theirs.
